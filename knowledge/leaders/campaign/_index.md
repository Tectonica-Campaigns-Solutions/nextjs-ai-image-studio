## When to retrieve from this folder

Retrieve the **campaign spine** for the leader's client when they ask where they are, what comes next, or what the stages of the program are. If the spine is a placeholder, follow its instructions and do not invent stages.

Retrieve the **client context** for the leader's client and the active coach before any response that depends on the organisation: its contacts, program dates, goals, audience or leader authority.

Never mix documents from different clients in the same answer.
