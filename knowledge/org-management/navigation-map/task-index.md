## TI — Task index

Vague requests mapped to the exact place, with the caveat that applies. This section is meant to be extended with real questions from staff.

### OM-01-TI-01 · "I need to set up my fundraising target"
→ Settings &gt; Goals & standards &gt; Fundraising target — /org/settings/fundraising
Caveat: pick the base currency first; set the amount in the card for your groups' currency (USD or GBP); currencies are never converted.
See: OM-01-ST-06

### OM-01-TI-02 · "A group has the wrong currency"
→ Groups &gt; group &gt; Edit details &gt; Group currency
Caveat: base currency in Settings only affects new groups.
See: OM-01-PG-GROUPS-03, OM-01-ST-06

### OM-01-TI-03 · "Set our recruitment goal"
→ Settings &gt; Goals & standards &gt; Member recruitment — /org/settings/member-goal
Caveat: it also drives the "far from goal" flag.
See: OM-01-ST-05, OM-01-ST-14

### OM-01-TI-04 · "Why is this group marked Quiet / Stalled / far from goal?"
→ Settings &gt; Operational &gt; Glance thresholds — /org/settings/operational-settings
Caveat: changes apply to every group.
See: OM-01-ST-14, OM-01-PG-GROUPS-01

### OM-01-TI-05 · "Approve a credential / badge"
→ Settings &gt; Approvals & queues &gt; Credential approvals — /org/settings/badge-queue — or the flags on Today and Groups.
Caveat: requests auto-grant when the window ends, default 7 days.
See: OM-01-ST-12

### OM-01-TI-06 · "Change the credential names or order"
→ Settings &gt; Approvals & queues &gt; Credential ladder — /org/settings/badge-definitions
Caveat: criteria are set by Support; switching a rung off withdraws its pending requests.
See: OM-01-ST-13

### OM-01-TI-07 · "Leaders' public pages need my approval"
→ Settings &gt; Approvals & queues &gt; Public sign-off, then Sign-off queue
Caveat: approving publishes immediately.
See: OM-01-ST-10, OM-01-ST-11

### OM-01-TI-08 · "Create a group"
→ Groups &gt; Add a group, or Settings &gt; Group lifecycle &gt; Create group
Caveat: creating a group emails an invite to its administrator.
See: OM-01-PG-GROUPS-01, OM-01-ST-07

### OM-01-TI-09 · "Pause / delete a group"
→ Settings &gt; Goals & standards &gt; Group lifecycle — /org/settings/group-lifecycle
Caveat: suspend is reversible; delete is permanent.
See: OM-01-ST-07

### OM-01-TI-10 · "Add / invite a new leader"
→ Leaders &gt; Add a leader, then Invite
Caveat: inviting creates a new group and sends the email.
See: OM-01-PG-LEADERS-03

### OM-01-TI-11 · "Import a list of prospects"
→ Leaders &gt; Import CSV
Caveat: no emails are sent; they start at Identified.
See: OM-01-PG-LEADERS-03

### OM-01-TI-12 · "Share our volunteer interest link / orientation QR"
→ Leaders &gt; Pipeline tools &gt; Public interest link / Orientation access
See: OM-01-PG-LEADERS-02

### OM-01-TI-13 · "Who is the point of contact for this leader?"
→ Leaders &gt; leader record &gt; Edit details &gt; Point of contact
Caveat: "My groups" on Groups filters by this.
See: OM-01-PG-LEADERS-04

### OM-01-TI-14 · "Message a leader"
→ Send &gt; New message, or the leader record &gt; Send a message
See: OM-01-PG-SEND-01

### OM-01-TI-15 · "Tell all leaders something"
→ Today &gt; What's happening for a feed announcement; Settings &gt; Leader communications &gt; Leader update for in-app with replies, Email to leaders for email, or Urgent message for one-way to all leaders.
Caveat: an urgent message cannot be undone.
See: OM-01-CT-04, OM-01-ST-20, OM-01-ST-21, OM-01-ST-22

### OM-01-TI-16 · "Record a video for my leaders"
→ Content &gt; Video updates &gt; Record a video, or Today &gt; Record today's announcement
Caveat: it needs camera and microphone permission.
See: OM-01-CT-07, OM-01-PG-TODAY-02

### OM-01-TI-17 · "Put something on leaders' home screen"
→ Content &gt; Briefing cards &gt; New card
Caveat: drafts are invisible; publish it.
See: OM-01-CT-01, OM-01-CT-02

### OM-01-TI-18 · "Build an onboarding sequence"
→ Content &gt; Journeys, plus briefing cards assigned to it
Caveat: each card must be published.
See: OM-01-CT-03

### OM-01-TI-19 · "Add an org event"
→ Content &gt; Organization events &gt; New event
Caveat: it goes to every group; for one group, post from What's happening.
See: OM-01-CT-05

### OM-01-TI-20 · "Share our logo / photos with groups"
→ Content &gt; Shared media &gt; Upload media
Caveat: choose All groups or Specific groups.
See: OM-01-CT-06

### OM-01-TI-21 · "Change our name or logo"
→ Settings &gt; Organization &gt; Identity — /org/settings/identity
See: OM-01-ST-03

### OM-01-TI-22 · "Add another admin"
→ Settings &gt; Organization &gt; Administrators — /org/settings/administrators
Caveat: admins can change every setting.
See: OM-01-ST-02

### OM-01-TI-23 · "Customize what new leaders agree to"
→ Settings &gt; Leader communications &gt; Welcome agreement
Caveat: leaders who already agreed keep their copy.
See: OM-01-ST-18

### OM-01-TI-24 · "Videos new leaders watch first"
→ Settings &gt; Leader communications &gt; Onboarding videos
Caveat: max 6; not live until saved.
See: OM-01-ST-19

### OM-01-TI-25 · "Starter tasks for leaders"
→ Settings &gt; Operational &gt; Suggested to-dos
Caveat: create a list first; unassigned tasks are invisible.
See: OM-01-ST-16

### OM-01-TI-26 · "Give groups work to do / a campaign task"
→ Work &gt; New assignment, or Objective / Task set
Caveat: it must be published and offered to groups.
See: OM-01-PG-WORK-02, OM-01-PG-WORK-03

### OM-01-TI-27 · "Where are our numbers / export a report?"
→ Numbers &gt; Export CSV — /org/reports
Caveat: funds need NationBuilder connected at Integrations.
See: OM-01-PG-NUM-01, OM-01-PG-INT-01

### OM-01-TI-28 · "See what a new leader experiences"
→ Settings &gt; Leader experience preview
Caveat: simulations only; no real data changes.
See: OM-01-ST-23

### OM-01-TI-29 · "What do I need to do today?" / "I'm lost"
→ Today: Today's briefing, Recommended today, Your to-dos
See: OM-01-PG-TODAY-01
